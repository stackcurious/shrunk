### One extra fix, outside the strict "presentation only" line

`MainTabsView` puts the window into `.dark` while the Scanner tab is selected,
and SwiftUI implements that as a window-level `overrideUserInterfaceStyle` — so
a sheet presented from that tab inherited it. **A light-mode user got a dark
Result screen from a scan and a light one from Browse.** This is restyle A's
deferred item 3 and it made the light App Store set impossible.

`SystemAppearance.current` (in `ShrunkTheme.swift`) reads the appearance off the
`UIScreen`, which is the one trait collection a window override does not touch —
the *scene's* does inherit it, which I established by shipping the scene version
first and watching shot 1 come back dark anyway. `ResultView` applies it as its
own `preferredColorScheme`. Camera chrome stays dark; a content screen follows
the device.

## The hero product changed twice — here is the audit trail

Shot 1 was Maxwell House. With the corrected curated barcode it returns a
**third** observation, Kroger-sourced, 27.5 oz — larger than the 2022 curated
24.5 oz — and `ShrinkDetector` compares the latest two, so the screen truthfully
reads **"Grew 12 %"** and adds a "Size unconfirmed" card.
`docs/APP_STORE_LISTING.md` requires shot 1 to show "a clear shrink verdict", so
this could not stand.

1. I re-pointed `featuredBarcode` at **Toblerone** (`7622210496645`) — two
   curated observations, no contradicting store row, −58.3 %. I also captured
   Tropicana (`0048500205716`, −18.8 %) and rejected it: Kroger lists it at a
   different size, so its Result screen carries the "Size unconfirmed" card,
   which pushes the live-price panel and the chart off the frame *and* puts "the
   size we're showing might be out of date" on the App Store hero.
2. Mid-capture, the agent re-sourcing the curated catalogue re-pointed it again,
   at **Gatorade** (`0052000135138`), on better evidence than I had: Mondelez
   **reverted** the 2016 Toblerone gap-widening in 2018, so today's bar is not
   the shrunk one. Their call is right and shots 1 and 2 were re-shot to match
   (`a3abd42`).

Gatorade is also simply the better screen: −12.5 % from two clean curated
points, no competing FDC or Kroger row to invert the verdict, Kroger listing it
at the curated after-size so there is no "Size unconfirmed" card, and a **real
6.0¢/oz under "Prices from Kroger"** — the first hero in this set that shows a
live price rather than "Store prices unavailable".

`SHRUNK_FEATURED_GTIN` still overrides the constant without a code change.
